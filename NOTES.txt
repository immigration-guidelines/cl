Chile: what was checked, what was saved, what is missing
=========================================================
Last full run: 15 November 2026.   Re-run:  python3 download_cl.py   (from the folder above cl/)

Short version
-------------
Chile has no single published manual for migration case officers. Guidance is issued as numbered
circulares, oficios (circulares) and resoluciones exentas, and it is published in four places, none of
them complete:

1. Diario Oficial / LeyChile: only the acts that must be gazetted (resolutions and decrees of general
   application). Obtained in full as LeyChile XML -> leychile/.
2. The old Departamento de Extranjería y Migración (DEM) site, extranjeria.gob.cl, closed in 2022: it
   listed about 40 circulars for 2017-2021. Only the Wayback Machine has them, and for a third of them
   its copy is missing or cut off at 1 MiB -> dem/ and not-obtained.tsv.
3. The Servicio Nacional de Migraciones (SERMIG) website: almost nothing. One page of oficios to other
   bodies and a few resolutions linked from news items -> sermig/.
4. SERMIG's Transparencia Activa pages on portaltransparencia.cl: THE place where the Service lists its
   own "circulares de uso interno" (25 of them, 2021-2026) and its protocol resolutions. Every document
   link there points to Google Drive. Drive's robots.txt allows only the viewer page, not the download
   address, so none of these was fetched. They are listed, with title, date and link, in
   not-obtained.tsv; each takes one click in a browser. A file saved by hand under the name given there
   is indexed with its title on the next run. This is the main gap of the collection.

Sources used
------------
- LeyChile (Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional)
    search:   https://nuevo.leychile.cl/servicios/Consulta/listaresultadosimple?cadena=...   (JSON, the
              service behind www.bcn.cl/leychile; 16 keyword searches)
    texts:    https://www.leychile.cl/Consulta/obtxml?opt=7&idNorma=N                        (documented XML)
    robots.txt: none (both hosts answer /robots.txt with an HTML page).
    Selection: resolutions, exempt decrees and decrees whose issuing body is the Ministerio del Interior
    (incl. Subsecretaría del Interior, DEM, SERMIG) or Relaciones Exteriores and whose title is about
    foreigners / migration / visas / refugees / travel documents. Left out on purpose: laws, the
    regulations themselves (Decreto 296/2021, Decreto 597/1984, Decreto 837/2010 and their amendments),
    treaties, staffing, delegation-of-signature, budget, councils and committees, "Sello Migrante",
    individual expulsions, and the 50-odd weekly decrees of 2020-21 extending the COVID border closure
    (Decreto 102/2020 series). The XML is the consolidated text; the date in the file name is the date of
    the act (promulgation), not of gazetting.
- Wayback Machine copies of www.extranjeria.gob.cl
    listing pages /legislacion-migratoria/ (2015-2019) and /leyes-migratorias/ (2019-2022), one capture
    per quarter, plus archived PDFs whose name says circular / oficio / instructivo / protocolo /
    resolución. robots.txt: none (404). Dates and numbers of the saved files were read from the documents
    (most are scans) and are written into the script's META table.
- https://serviciomigraciones.cl  (robots.txt: none, 404; WordPress REST API is switched off)
    all 665 pages in the page, guia and post sitemaps were read once; later runs re-read only pages whose
    sitemap date changed. Documents: /comunicaciones-y-oficios/ (9 oficios), /aranceles-migratorios/
    (fee resolution 129.194), and two resolutions linked from a news item.
    /normativa-migratoria/ lists only laws and decrees, all by link to LeyChile.
- https://www.portaltransparencia.cl/PortalPdT/directorio-de-organismos-regulados/?org=AB099
    robots.txt allows these pages and asks for Crawl-delay: 60, which is kept. Read: "02 Marco Normativo"
    (27 entries) and "07 Actos y resoluciones con efectos sobre terceros > Circulares" (25 entries).

Checked and not used
--------------------
- portaltransparencia.cl "01 Actos publicados en el Diario Oficial" (67 entries): the same acts as
  LeyChile, mostly staffing; links go to Google Drive or interior.gob.cl.
- portaltransparencia.cl section 07, other sub-lists ("Reconocimiento de derechos", "Resoluciones
  Departamento de Refugio", "Histórico SERMIG", "Rechazos", ...): individual case decisions. Out of scope.
- www.interior.gob.cl/transparencia/ (Subsecretaría del Interior, where pre-2021 DEM acts were listed):
  answers scripts with a JavaScript bot check. Not used.
- www.diariooficial.interior.gob.cl: robots.txt allows the "normas generales" PDFs but not the search or
  the editions, so there is no permitted way to find them by script; LeyChile has the same texts.
- drive.google.com / drive.usercontent.google.com: robots.txt disallows the download addresses.
- extranjeria.gob.cl itself: no longer answers (time-out; www has a certificate for another name).
- Left out as applicant material: the TLC "guides for business persons" (manual_mexico, manual_tlc_*),
  the Antofagasta "instructivo" for applying by post, forms, leaflets, statistics, COSOC minutes,
  the Dirección del Trabajo oficio 843/2023 and Contraloría dictamen D75/2026 linked from SERMIG news
  (not issued by the migration authority).

Things that went wrong
----------------------
- The Wayback Machine refused connections ("connection refused", HTTP 503) on and off during the run.
  The script paces it at 8 s and stops asking after three failures in a row; it took several runs to get
  through the list. Items it definitely cannot supply are remembered in listings/dem-unavailable.json
  and not asked for again (delete that file to retry).
- Several DEM circulars exist in the archive only as a capture cut off at exactly 1,048,576 bytes.
  Those are broken PDFs and were not kept.

Completeness doubts
-------------------
- SERMIG circulars: none held (see point 4). The portal list itself starts in mid-2021 and skips
  numbers, so even that list is not the full series.
- DEM circulars before 2017 were never on the website, apart from Oficio Circular 26465 (2009) and
  Circular 30.722 (2014, archive copy cut off). Older ones are known only from citations.
- Refugee procedure: no circular or instructivo on refugee status determination was found published
  anywhere; only the law, its regulation and Ley 21.655 (2024).
- LeyChile search is by keyword; an act with an unusual title could be missed.
- Ministerio de Relaciones Exteriores consular instructions on visas (e.g. the 2018 oficio circular on
  the "visa de responsabilidad democrática") are not published.
