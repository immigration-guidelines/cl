# Immigration guidance: Chile

Published instructions, circulars and resolutions that Chile's migration authorities issue for the officials who decide visa, residence and expulsion cases, collected in November 2026.

- **80 documents**, 1970 to 2026. `index.tsv` lists each with its title, date, issuing body, act number, source address and an English gloss of the title.
- `dem/`: 26 circulars, oficios and instructions of the former Departamento de Extranjería y Migración, 2008 to 2021, from archived copies of its closed website.
- `sermig/`: 12 oficios and resolutions of the Servicio Nacional de Migraciones, 2020 to 2023, from its own site.
- `leychile/`: 42 gazetted resolutions and decrees that officers work from (residence subcategories, return protocols, biometric registration, criteria for definitive residence), as XML from the Library of Congress's legal database.
- **The main gap:** the migration service's own circulars since 2021 are not here. It lists them on the government transparency portal with links to Google Drive only, and Drive's robots.txt disallows automated download. `not-obtained.tsv` gives the link for each of the 26, and the file name to save it under, so they can be added by hand; contributions are welcome.
- `NOTES.txt` records what was checked. For all jurisdictions see [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md) and the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
